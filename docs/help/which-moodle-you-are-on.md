UACH runs **two separate Moodle installations**. They are not one site with two addresses.

| host | what it is called | Moodle version |
|---|---|---|
| `virtual3.uach.mx` | Campus Virtual | 3.3 |
| `aulas1.uach.mx` | Aulas Virtuales | 4.5 |

Three major versions apart. The menus are in different places, the settings pages are laid out
differently, and some screens exist on one and not the other.

## Why this matters for the help you are reading

A procedure that is right for 4.5 and wrong for 3.3, delivered confidently, is worse than no
answer at all: you follow it and end up looking for a menu that does not exist, with no way to
tell whether you misread the instruction or the instruction was wrong.

So every help topic here declares which Moodle it applies to, and the assistant only offers you
the ones that match the courses you actually teach on. If you are told a procedure does not
apply to your Moodle, that is the filter doing its job — not a gap.

## Which one are your courses on?

MUSAI records it per course when it maps them, and it uses that host every time it opens a
course. If your courses are on Campus Virtual, you will be given 3.3 procedures; if on Aulas
Virtuales, 4.5 ones.

Access is per host and separate. Having an account on one does not give you anything on the
other.

## If you have courses on both

You will see topics for both, each labelled. Check the label before following one.
