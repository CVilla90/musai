Restore a backup into it

Uploads a .mbz and restores it into {{ course.group_code }}.

{# 🔴 Said plainly, before the file picker rather than after it. This is Moodle's own wizard behaviour ("Eliminar los contenidos de este curso y después restaurar"), and it is what makes a copy behave like a copy. #}
This replaces everything in the course. Activities, pages and the gradebook are deleted first, then the backup is restored over them. Enrolled students stay enrolled, but anything they have submitted or been graded on goes with the old contents.

Nothing is restored yet. MUSAI reads the file, opens the course and tells you exactly what would be replaced — about thirty seconds. You confirm after that.
